## Overall Progress

Based on the roadmap, you've planned **11 core milestones** and identified **9 additional advanced milestones**, for a total of **20 major milestones**.

* **Core ERP (Milestones 1–11):** ~55% of the complete vision. This is enough to deliver a professional, sellable desktop application for most auto parts businesses.
* **Advanced Platform (Milestones 12–20):** ~45% of the vision. These features differentiate the product from typical inventory systems and move it toward an enterprise-grade platform.

Once Milestones **1–11** are implemented, Inventory Gear should already be competitive with many commercial desktop inventory and POS systems used by automotive parts stores. The remaining milestones focus on differentiation, scalability, AI, integrations, and enterprise capabilities.
