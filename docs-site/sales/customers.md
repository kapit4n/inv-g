# Sales Customers

## What is it?

A read-only list of every customer registered in the system, shown from inside the
Sales module. It answers the question "who are our customers?" without leaving
Sales, so you can confirm a customer exists before you attach them to an invoice.

## What is it for?

- Verify a customer was registered correctly (name, email, phone, city) before
  creating a sale.
- See at a glance how many customers are active vs. archived.
- Quickly reach a customer's full record.

This is a **viewing** page. To create or edit customers, use
[Manage Customers](/crm/customers) or the quick-add dialog inside
[Point of Sale](/sales/pos).

## How to Access

**Sales → Customers**

## How to Use

### Step 1: Review the summary cards

Four cards at the top summarize the list:

| Card | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Total Customers | Every customer on record, active and archived |
| Active | Customers that can be attached to a sale |
| Inactive | Archived customers |
| New this month | Customers registered since the first day of the current month |

### Step 2: Search

Type in the search box to filter by name, email, or phone. The list refreshes
as you type.

### Step 3: Open a customer

Click any row to open the customer's full record, where you can review their
purchase history, notes, and credit account.

## Considerations

- Inactive (archived) customers remain listed so historical invoices still
  resolve to a name, but they should not receive new sales.
- The list shows only customers. It does not show per-customer sales totals —
  use [Sales History](/sales/history) for that.
- Search matches name, email, and phone only.
