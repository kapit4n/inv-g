# Quotes

## What is it?

Quotes let you prepare price estimates for customers before committing to a sale.

![Quotes](/screenshots/light/17-quotes.png)

## What is it for?

Create professional quotes for customers who need time to decide or require approval before purchasing.

## How to Access

**Sidebar → Sales → Quotes**. Route: `/sales/quotes`.

## How to Create a Quote

### Step 1: Go to Quotes

Click **Quotes** in the sidebar.

### Step 2: Click "New Quote"

Click the **+ New Quote** button.

### Step 3: Add Products

Search and add products just like in the POS.

### Step 4: Set Customer

Assign a customer to the quote.

### Step 5: Save

Click **Save** to store the quote.

## Quote Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Draft | Being prepared |
| Sent | Sent to customer |
| Accepted | Customer approved |
| Rejected | Customer declined |
| Expired | Past validity period |

## Converting Quote to Sale

1. Open the quote
2. Click **Convert to Sale**
3. The POS opens with all items pre-loaded
4. Complete the sale normally
