# Supplier Products & Costs

## What is it?

Manage supplier catalogs, track product costs over time, and get reorder suggestions based on stock levels.

## Sub-modules

| Module | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| Supplier Products | `/purchases/supplier-products` | Products per supplier |
| Cost History | `/purchases/cost-history` | Price change tracking |
| Reorder Suggestions | `/purchases/reorder-suggestions` | Auto-reorder alerts |

## Supplier Products

### What are they?

A catalog of products each supplier offers, with their specific pricing and lead times.

### How to Add

1. Go to **Purchases → Supplier Products**
2. Click **+ New**
3. Select **Supplier** and **Product**
4. Enter **Supplier SKU**, **Unit Cost**, and **Lead Time**
5. Save

## Cost History

### What is it?

Tracks cost price changes for each product over time.

### How to Access

**Purchases → Cost History**

View historical costs, identify price trends, and make informed purchasing decisions.

## Reorder Suggestions

### What is it?

Automatic suggestions for products that need reordering based on:
- Current stock vs. reorder point
- Sales velocity
- Supplier lead times

### How to Use

1. Go to **Purchases → Reorder Suggestions**
2. Review suggested products
3. Select items to order
4. Create a Purchase Order directly from suggestions
