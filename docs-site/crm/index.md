# CRM & Vehicles

## What is it?

The CRM (Customer Relationship Management) module manages customers, vehicles, compatibility data, service reminders, warranties, credit accounts, and communication notes.

![CRM Dashboard](/screenshots/light/33-crm-dashboard.png)

## Sub-modules

| Module | Route | Purpose |
|--------|-------|---------|
| [Dashboard](/crm/) | `/crm` | CRM overview |
| [Customers](/crm/customers) | `/crm/customers` | Customer database |
| [Vehicles & Compatibility](/crm/vehicles) | `/crm/vehicles` | Vehicle-to-part mapping |
| [Reminders & Warranties](/crm/reminders-warranties) | `/crm/reminders` | Service and warranty tracking |
| [Credit & Notes](/crm/credit-notes) | `/crm/credit` | Customer credit and notes |

## CRM Dashboard

The CRM dashboard shows:
- Total customers
- Active vehicles
- Upcoming service reminders
- Expiring warranties
- Credit summary
