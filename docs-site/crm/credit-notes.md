# Credit & Notes

## What is it?

Manage customer credit accounts (buy-now-pay-later) and maintain communication notes.

## Customer Credit

### What is it?

Allow approved customers to make purchases on credit, with balances tracked per customer.

### How to Access

**Sidebar → CRM → Credit**. Route: `/crm/credit`.

### How it Works

1. **Credit Account** — Created for approved customers with a credit limit
2. **Credit Sales** — POS sales charged to credit account
3. **Payments** — Customers make payments against their balance
4. **Balance** — Running balance tracked automatically

### Credit Account Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Credit Limit | Maximum amount allowed |
| Current Balance | Amount owed |
| Payment Terms | Days to pay (e.g., Net 30) |
| Status | Active, Suspended, Closed |

### Making a Credit Payment

1. Open customer detail
2. Go to **Credit** tab
3. Click **Add Payment**
4. Enter amount and payment method
5. Save

## Customer Notes

### What are They?

Internal notes and communication logs for each customer.

### How to Access

**Sidebar → CRM → Notes**. Route: `/crm/notes`.

### Types of Notes

| Type | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| General | Internal observations |
| Communication | Phone calls, emails, meetings |
| Service | Service-related notes |
| Credit | Credit-related notes |

### Adding a Note

1. Open a customer's detail page
2. Click the **Notes** tab
3. Click **+ Add Note**
4. Select **Type** and enter content
5. Save
