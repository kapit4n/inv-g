# Categories & Brands

## What is it?

Categories and Brands help organize your product catalog for easy navigation, filtering, and reporting.

![Categories](/screenshots/light/07-category-list.png)

## Categories

### What are Categories?

Categories group similar products together (e.g., Brakes, Engine, Electrical, Suspension, Filters).

### How to Create a Category

1. Go to **Inventory → Categories**
2. Click **+ New Category**
3. Enter a **Name** (required)
4. Optionally add a **Description** and **Parent Category** (for subcategories)
5. Click **Save**

### How to Edit a Category

1. Find the category in the list
2. Click the **Edit** action
3. Modify and save

### How to Archive a Category

1. Click the **Archive** action on the category
2. Products in that category remain but are uncategorized

::: warning
Archiving a category does not delete its products.
:::

## Brands

### What are Brands?

Brands represent the manufacturers or brands of products you carry (e.g., Bosch, Monroe, ACDelco, Denso).

### How to Create a Brand

1. Go to **Inventory → Brands**
2. Click **+ New Brand**
3. Enter a **Name** (required)
4. Optionally add a **Description**
5. Click **Save**

### How to Edit a Brand

1. Find the brand in the list
2. Click the **Edit** action
3. Modify and save

## Considerations

- Use consistent naming for categories and brands
- Categories support hierarchical structure (parent/child)
- Brands help with product search and reporting
- Archived categories/brands can be restored
