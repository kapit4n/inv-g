# First Steps

After logging in for the first time, here's how to get started with Inventory Gear.

## 1. Explore the Dashboard

The **Dashboard** is your business overview. Take a moment to understand:

- **Stats cards** — Today's sales, revenue, active orders
- **Charts** — Revenue trends, category breakdown
- **Stock alerts** — Products that need reordering
- **Recent activity** — Latest sales and purchases

![Dashboard](/screenshots/light/02-dashboard.png)

## 2. Set Up Your Business

Before using the system, configure these foundational elements:

### Categories
Organize your products into categories (e.g., Brakes, Engine, Electrical).
→ [Inventory → Categories & Brands](/inventory/categories-brands)

### Brands
Add the brands you carry (e.g., Bosch, Monroe, ACDelco).
→ [Inventory → Categories & Brands](/inventory/categories-brands)

### Warehouses
Define your storage locations.
→ [Inventory → Warehouses & Locations](/inventory/warehouses)

### Suppliers
Add your suppliers with contact information.
→ [Purchasing → Supplier Products & Costs](/purchases/supplier-products)

## 3. Add Products

Create your first products:
- Name, SKU, barcode
- OEM numbers, internal codes
- Pricing (cost, sale, wholesale)
- Stock levels

→ [Inventory → Products](/inventory/products)

## 4. Make a Sale

Process your first transaction:
1. Go to **Sales → Point of Sale**
2. Search for a product (by name, SKU, or barcode)
3. Add to cart
4. Select payment method
5. Complete the sale

→ [Sales → Point of Sale](/sales/pos)

## 5. Create a Purchase Order

Order stock from suppliers:
1. Go to **Purchases → Orders**
2. Create a new purchase order
3. Add products and quantities
4. Save and send to supplier

→ [Purchasing → Purchase Orders](/purchases/orders)
