# Getting Started

Welcome to **Inventory Gear** — a desktop ERP system designed specifically for automotive parts businesses.

## What is Inventory Gear?

Inventory Gear is a complete business management solution that helps you:

- **Manage inventory** — Track products, stock levels, warehouses, and movements
- **Process sales** — Fast point-of-sale with barcode scanning, quotes, and returns
- **Handle purchasing** — Purchase orders, receiving, supplier management
- **Manage customers & vehicles** — CRM with vehicle compatibility tracking
- **Generate reports** — Sales analytics, inventory reports, profitability tracking
- **Control access** — Role-based permissions for your team

## System Requirements

- **Operating System:** Windows 10+, macOS 10.15+, or Linux (Ubuntu 20.04+)
- **RAM:** 4 GB minimum, 8 GB recommended
- **Storage:** 500 MB free disk space
- **Display:** 1200×700 minimum resolution

## Installation

1. Download the latest release for your platform
2. Run the installer:
   - **Windows:** Double-click the `.exe` file
   - **macOS:** Drag the `.app` to Applications
   - **Linux:** Install the `.deb` or `.AppImage`
3. Launch Inventory Gear from your applications

## First Launch

When you first open Inventory Gear:

1. The **login screen** will appear
2. Use the default credentials or ask your administrator
3. The **dashboard** will load with your business overview

::: tip
The application comes pre-loaded with sample data for testing. In production, your administrator will configure the system with your actual business data.
:::
