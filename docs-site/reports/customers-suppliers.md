# Customer & Supplier Reports

## What is it?

Analytics for customer behavior and supplier performance.

## Customer Reports

| Report | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Customer Ranking | Top customers by revenue |
| Customer Growth | New customers over time |
| Customer Location | Geographic distribution |
| Credit Summary | Outstanding credit balances |
| Service Summary | Reminder and warranty stats |

## Supplier Reports

| Report | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Supplier Ranking | Top suppliers by purchase volume |
| Lead Time Analysis | Delivery time per supplier |
| Cost Trends | Price changes over time |

## How to Access

- **Customers:** Sidebar → Reports → Customers
- **Suppliers:** Sidebar → Reports → Suppliers
