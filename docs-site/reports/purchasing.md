# Purchasing & Profitability Reports

## What is it?

Analytics for procurement performance, cost analysis, and business profitability.

## Purchasing Reports

| Report | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Purchase Summary | Total purchases, order count |
| Purchases by Supplier | Spend per supplier |
| Purchase by Category | Procurement by product category |
| PO Status Summary | Draft, sent, received counts |
| Supplier Ranking | Top suppliers by volume |
| Lead Time Analysis | Average supplier delivery times |

## Profitability Reports

| Report | Content |
|--------|---------|
| Profit by Product | Margin per product |
| Profit by Category | Margin per category |
| Profit by Supplier | Margin on supplier products |
| Profit Trend | Profit over time |

## How to Access

- **Purchasing:** Sidebar → Reports → Purchasing
- **Profitability:** Sidebar → Reports → Profitability
